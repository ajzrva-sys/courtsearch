Privacy policy

This policy describes the Court search app for Android. It looks up public case information from the Virginia General District Court Online Case Information System at eapps.courts.state.va.us.

Information you type

When you search, the last name, first name, and middle initial you enter are sent to that court website so it can run the search. The app does not send them to the developer.

Information kept on your phone

Search results, case details the app was able to load, and the list of previous searches stay in storage on your phone. The app does not upload that store to the developer.

The app does not keep date of birth, gender, or race, even when a court page shows them.

PDFs are created on your phone. Sharing or printing a PDF uses another app you choose.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses one to show progress while a search is running. That notification is not used for marketing.

No account, ads, or sale of data

The app has no account, no advertising, and no analytics. The developer does not sell your information.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Contact

Use the email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
